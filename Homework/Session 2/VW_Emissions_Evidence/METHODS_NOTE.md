# Methods note: what the WVU/ICCT study measured, and its limits

Page numbers are the report's printed pages (PDF page = printed page + 16 from Chapter 1).

## Why the study was done

ICCT commissioned West Virginia University (WVU) after European research found off-cycle
NOx from diesel cars 4–7 times above Euro limits (introduction, p. 1). The stated objective
was to measure real-world NOx from U.S. Tier 2 Bin 5 diesels and compare NOx-control
technologies (p. 2). The report does not say the aim was to promote or to investigate any
manufacturer.

## Instrument: portable emissions measurement system (PEMS)

A PEMS travels with the car and measures exhaust gas concentrations and exhaust flow while it
is driven, so pollutant mass per kilometre can be computed on real roads. WVU used a Horiba
OBS-2200 for NOx, CO, CO2 and hydrocarbons, and a Pegasor PPS-M particle sensor (pp. ii, 2).

The PEMS was cross-checked against the laboratory CVS system on **Vehicle B only** (§3.3.3.3,
p. 50). Agreement was good for CO2 but not perfect for NOx: over the weighted FTP-75 the CVS
and PEMS NOx results differed by **18.8 %** (0.018 vs 0.015 g/km, Table 3.13, p. 55). That is
small next to the 15–35-fold road excesses, but it is not "single-digit agreement".

## Limits that affect the conclusion

1. **Three vehicles are three case studies, not a sample.** The report says its conclusions
   "are confined to these three vehicles" (Executive Summary, p. iv). No fleet-wide rate can
   be inferred.
2. **Road routes are not certification tests.** The 0.043 g/km standard is a certification
   benchmark used as a reference; "15–35×" means road mean ÷ standard, not road ÷ lab.
3. **Lab vs road conditions differ.** The weighted FTP-75 includes a cold start; road runs
   were warm (pp. ii–iii, 57). The report notes cold starts usually raise NOx, so this does
   not inflate the road-to-lab gap.
4. **Small repeat counts.** Standard deviations come from 1–3 runs; single runs have none.
   Table 3.15 lists three runs for C on Route 3, but Table 4.3's caption says SDs are over two
   runs; this unresolved inconsistency is disclosed, and the published mean and SD are used.
5. **Cause was not established by the study.** WVU discussed control strategy, SCR sizing,
   urea dosing and filter regeneration (pp. 63–64). Test-detecting software and intent were
   established later by the EPA notice (pp. 3–4) and VW's admitted Statement of Facts
   (plea agreement, Exh. 2 ¶¶ 33–36, 49–58).
